Principal Investigator

Dr. Lekhnath Sharma Pathak

Assistant Professor · Central Department of Linguistics, Tribhuvan University

PhD Cognitive Science · University of Hyderabad

Psycholinguistics · Cognitive Science · Pedagogy · Linguistics

Dr. Pathak founded Nepal's first Cognitive Science & Psycholinguistics Lab. His PhD from the Center for Neural & Cognitive Sciences at the University of Hyderabad (under Ramesh Kumar Mishra) focused on bilingual language processing. His research spans child language acquisition, multilingual cognition, dyslexia, psycholinguistic approaches to honorification, and cross-linguistic spatial semantics.

Beyond the lab, Dr. Pathak has advocated for cognitive science as an academic discipline in Nepal. In February 2020, he delivered the keynote “Situation of Illiterates in Nepal: What Does It Mean for Cognitive Science?” at the Third International Workshop of the Society for Cognitive Science of Culture at BITS Pilani, Goa. He also undertook an SNSF Scientific Visit to the University of Zurich.

Researchers

Mohan Gurung

MPhil–PhD Scholar · Journalist
Central Department of Linguistics, Tribhuvan University

Mohan Gurung holds master’s degrees in Population Studies and Media Technology and is a practising journalist who has worked with several media houses. Under the supervision of Dr. Lekhnath Sharma Pathak, he studies Gurung language acquisition and intergenerational transmission. His work forms part of a research collaboration with the Institute for the Interdisciplinary Study of Language Evolution at the University of Zürich.

Sambit Rimal

MA Linguistics Student · Student Lab Manager
Central Department of Linguistics, Tribhuvan University

Sambit Rimal is an MA Linguistics student at the Central Department of Linguistics, Tribhuvan University, and CSPL’s Student Lab Manager. He has a background in Computer Science and Information Technology and is completing a Higher Diploma in Writing and Research at The Open Institute, with a focus on science and technology studies (STS). His current research uses eye-tracking and mouse-tracking to investigate trilingual parallel language activation, cognitive control and agent preference in visual event apprehension. He was a 2025 CogSci × Gorilla Grant recipient.

2024 · Cross-Cultural Psycholinguistics and Cognitive Science Workshop Measuring Cognitive Control using Mouse-tracing and Eye-tracking, University of Zurich & Tribhuvan University.

Sabita Rijal

Architect. Psycholinguistics and Cognitive Science Researcher. Literacy and Cognition

Sabita Rijal is an architect who completed an MA in Linguistics at Tribhuvan University. For her master’s thesis, supervised by Dr. Pathak, she investigated how first- and second-language-mediated instruction relates to cognitive control. The study used linguistic measures alongside Stroop and Flanker tasks in a mouse-tracking paradigm. During her free time, she enjoys painting, hiking, reading and listening to music.

Aarya Pathak

Research Intern · Computer Science Undergraduate
School of Computer Science and Engineering, Kathmandu University

Aarya Pathak works on technology-assisted approaches to cognition, language and mental health. At CSPL, he is developing AR-assisted vocabulary-learning tools for children with autism and exploring how AI may support people with speech and language difficulties. He is also involved in Cognify, a platform combining cognitive tools and natural language processing for mental-health support. His team won the national Code for Impact hackathon and received seed funding for its AR vocabulary-learning project, followed by startup mentoring and incubation at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras. In his free time he enjoys reading, hiking, cycling and endurance training.
