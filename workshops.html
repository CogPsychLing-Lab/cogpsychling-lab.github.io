2026

Invited programme

Language, Mind, and Society — Invited Lecture & Mouse-Tracking Workshop

August 8 & 10, 2026 · School of Languages and Linguistics, Jadavpur University · Dr. Lekhnath Sharma Pathak

Dr. Pathak delivered an invited lecture on bilingual and trilingual parallel language activation on August 8. A hands-on workshop on August 10 will introduce mouse tracking for experimental design, data collection, analysis, and interpretation in language-processing research.

  • Bilingual processing
  • Trilingual activation
  • Mouse tracking
  • Experimental design

Methods training

Introduction to PsychoPy & EEG for Cognitive Science & Psycholinguistics

February 6–8, 2026 · Central Dept. of Linguistics, TU · Dr. Laura Giglio (University of Zurich)

A 3-day hands-on workshop introducing key methods in psycholinguistics. Participants gained practical experience in designing experiments with PsychoPy, collecting EEG data, and analyzing results using R and MATLAB.

  • Experimental design
  • PsychoPy
  • EEG methodology
  • Behavioral analysis
  • Neural data analysis

Supported by the University of Zurich and NCCR Evolving Language.

2021

International webinar

Cognitive Science and Psycholinguistics for Understanding Human Mind, Brain and Cognition

February 26, 2021 · Online · CSPL · NTNU Alumni Nepal · NTNU Language Acquisition and Language Processing Lab

Dr. Pathak initiated this international webinar on the prospects and possibilities of cognitive science and psycholinguistics in Nepal. It brought together researchers, university leaders, mental-health professionals, and representatives of the Royal Norwegian Embassy.

2020

Foundational keynote & research presentation

Third International Workshop of the Society for Cognitive Science of Culture

February 3–7, 2020 · BITS Pilani, Goa

Dr. Pathak delivered the keynote “Situation of Illiterates in Nepal: What Does It Mean for Cognitive Science?” Sabita Rijal and Dr. Pathak also presented “Bilingual Literacy Effect on Executive Control,” foundational experimental work that preceded the lab’s formal establishment in 2021.
